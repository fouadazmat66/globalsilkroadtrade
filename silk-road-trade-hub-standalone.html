    Trade terms

    The words you will meet in almost every deal, in plain English.

    Incoterms
    Standard rules that say who pays for shipping, insurance and risk at each stage. Examples: EXW (buyer handles almost everything), FOB (seller delivers goods onto the ship), CIF (seller pays freight and insurance to the destination port).
    Letter of credit (L/C)
    A bank's promise to pay the seller once the seller shows the agreed documents. It protects both sides.
    HS code
    A number that classifies a product for customs. It decides the duty you pay, so ask your supplier and your customs agent to confirm it.
    Bill of lading
    The document a carrier issues for your shipment. It is a receipt, a contract of carriage, and usually proof of ownership.
    Certificate of origin
    Shows which country the goods were made in. It can lower duties under a trade agreement.
    MOQ
    Minimum order quantity: the smallest order a supplier will accept.
